Understanding the conditions

Learn about each condition, its general characteristics, and symptoms that may require medical attention.

Diabetes

Diabetes mellitus describes conditions that affect how the body regulates blood glucose. Glucose is an important energy source, and persistently elevated blood sugar can lead to serious health complications.

Common symptoms
  • Increased thirst
  • Frequent urination
  • Extreme hunger
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Blurred vision

Heart / Cardiovascular Disease

Cardiovascular diseases affect the heart and blood vessels. Some conditions cause noticeable symptoms, while others may develop without obvious warning signs.

Possible symptoms
  • Heart palpitations
  • Chest pain
  • Sweating
  • Lightheadedness
  • Shortness of breath

Breast Cancer

Breast cancer occurs when cells in breast tissue grow abnormally. It can affect women and men, although it is much more common in women. Early assessment of unusual breast changes is important.

Possible warning signs
  • New breast or underarm lump
  • Breast thickening
  • Skin dimpling
  • Nipple or skin changes
  • Nipple-area pain

Chronic Kidney Disease

Chronic kidney disease (CKD) involves gradual damage that reduces the kidneys' ability to filter waste and excess fluid from the blood. Symptoms may be absent in early stages.

Possible symptoms
  • Nausea
  • Vomiting
  • Fatigue and weakness
  • Muscle cramps
  • Loss of appetite

Liver Disease

Liver disease includes several conditions that can damage liver tissue and impair its function. Some long-term conditions can cause scarring and may lead to severe complications if untreated.

Possible symptoms
  • Abdominal pain
  • Easy bruising
  • Urine or stool color changes
  • Fatigue
  • Nausea or vomiting

Important: Medibuddy is an educational machine-learning project. The displayed accuracy values are reported project metrics and do not establish clinical reliability. Predictions and symptom information must not replace professional medical evaluation or diagnosis.
